Clear the whole sphere

Every number tells you how many mines touch that cell. Combine the clues until only the mines remain.

  1. Tap a cell to reveal it. Your first reveal and its neighbors are protected.
  2. Hold to mark a suspected mine, or rotate the board to inspect another side.
  3. Reveal every safe cell without touching a mine.
